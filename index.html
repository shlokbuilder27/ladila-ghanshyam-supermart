FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Dabholi Store Help & Policies

Everything you need to know about our daily ordering, delivery radius & quality guarantees.

Q. Which societies in Dabholi do you deliver to?

We deliver daily to Bhoomidarshan Society, Kailash Nagar Society, Shiv Nagar, Singanpor Road, Ved Road, and all neighboring residential complexes near Dabholi Char Rasta within a 2.5 km radius.

Q. What is the minimum order for free doorstep delivery?

Delivery is 100% FREE for all orders above ₹300. For orders under ₹300, a nominal delivery fee of ₹20 applies. You can also opt for our free 15-Minute Store Counter Pickup if you are walking past Dabholi Char Rasta.

Q. How do I make the payment?

You pay only after receiving your items! We accept UPI on Delivery (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) via the delivery partner's QR code, or direct Cash on Delivery (COD).

Q. Can I type custom items not listed on the website?

Yes! Use the "Custom Monthly Kirana List" box inside the receipt drawer. You can type or paste items in English or Gujarati (e.g., "5kg Sharbati Gehu, 1L Gulab Singtel, 2 Dettol Soap"), and our shop team will bill and pack them together.

Q. What is your policy on unsealed or damaged products?

We stand by 100% customer trust. If any packet is damaged or unsatisfactory, simply inform the delivery partner or message us on WhatsApp for an instant on-the-spot replacement or full refund.
